# Buddy Loans landing page

## Confirmed direction
Mobile web application. Premium, Apple-inspired simplicity. Carefully composed typography, brand colours, distinctive graphics, and purposeful motion.

## Brand palette
Approximate colours interpreted from Premium Student Loan App Showcase.png:
- Deep navy: #091222 — primary text and dark surfaces.
- Coral red: #FF2948 — brand accent.
- Warm orange: #FF813B — secondary accent and gradient endpoint.
- Cool off-white: #F8F9FC — page background.
- Pale grey: #EEF0F6 — secondary surfaces.
- Muted slate: #68738B — secondary text.
- White: #FFFFFF — elevated surfaces.

Use navy for readable button labels on bright accents. Keep coral-to-orange gradients concentrated in graphics and selective highlights.

## Visitor journey
1. Understand what Buddy Loans does and who it helps.
2. See a concrete demonstration of the core experience.
3. Understand how the service works, including the next step.
4. Resolve practical questions before committing.

## Visual direction
Manrope for headlines and interface text, with a system sans-serif fallback. Strong hierarchy, generous negative space, a compact mobile header, and comfortably sized touch targets. Brand colours are centralised in foundation.css. Graphics should explain the confirmed product rather than rely on a stretched phone mockup.

## Motion
Use short opacity and transform entrances, tactile button feedback, and a product demonstration controlled by the visitor. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep reading and scrolling unobstructed.
