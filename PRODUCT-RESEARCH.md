# Buddy Loans: product, assessment, pricing and marketing

Research date: 25 October 2026. This is a working product brief, not a signed-off lending or tax opinion. Confirm the agreement classification, full current legal position and calculation methodology with a South African credit-law practitioner before offering credit. Statutory ceilings are not a recommendation to charge the maximum.

## Confirmed by the founder
University students; loans up to R700; student-email sign-up; affordability and credit checks; ID, proof of registration, proof of residence and latest three months of bank statements; one repayment on the next verified allowance date, collected through an authorised debit-order mandate. The founder confirmed NCR registration is still to be done. AI-assisted statement analysis is proposed, not implemented. Keep real applications and lending closed until the required registration and operational setup are complete.

## Marketing research and decisions
Capitec separates eligibility, documents and costs, including advising customers to compare total repayment rather than just interest. Barko presents a short application journey. Revolut leads with a concise benefit and a clear next action. These are observed patterns, not evidence that their exact wording will improve Buddy Loans conversions.

For Buddy Loans, lead with: “A little support. For student life.” Follow immediately with “Short-term loans up to R700 for university students. Subject to affordability and credit checks.”

Keep the current premium palette and motion. Replace vague brand-only messaging with useful product detail: maximum amount; who can apply; what is assessed; documents; the proposed repayment arrangement; and an itemised quote before acceptance. While applications are not open, label the planned offer and use “See requirements” rather than a fake application button.

Do not claim guaranteed approval, no credit checks, instant payouts, university/NSFAS affiliation, existing bureau/payment partnerships, or NCR registration without evidence. Avoid presenting borrowing as a way to build wealth or as a reward for repeat usage. No university logos without permission.

Sources: [Capitec](https://www.capitecbank.co.za/personal/credit/how-to-qualify-for-credit/), [Barko](https://www.barko.co.za/), [Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/).

## Interest and fees: working legal position
Assuming the product is a short-term credit transaction, the researched Regulation 42 limits are 5% per month on the first loan and 3% per month on subsequent loans within a calendar year. Confirm how the relevant prior-loan history must be established; do not reset the repeat-loan rate merely because a user changes an account or email.

The short-term initiation schedule is R165 plus 10% of the amount above R1,000, subject to an R1,050 ceiling AND Regulation 43(3)'s 15% of principal-debt limit. For principal debt of R700, that percentage is R105. Do not automatically use R165. Confirm the statutory principal-debt basis if fees are financed.

The service-fee ceiling is R60 per month before applicable VAT. It covers administration, including banking and repayment processing. It must be pro-rated where the agreement is concluded during a calendar month. An initiation fee applies when a new agreement is established, not on every transaction under one agreement.

VAT, if lawfully applicable to taxable fees by a VAT-registered supplier, is currently 15%. Thus R105 plus VAT is R120.75 and R60 plus VAT is R69. These are conditional fee illustrations, not a final R700 repayment quote. Do not add VAT to interest or collect VAT as a non-vendor. Obtain tax confirmation for the actual structure.

The final total depends on actual dates, interest principal, whether any fee is financed, calendar-month service fees, lawful pro-rating and VAT status. Do not implement “R700 × 5% + fixed fees” as a universal quote. Do not invent extra AI, verification, application or debit-order administration charges to bypass limits. Any default/collection charge needs its own statutory basis and controls.

Sources: [2015 final fee/interest regulations](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201511/39379gon1080.pdf); [NCR's published review terms retaining the 5%/3% framework](https://ncr.org.za/index.php/component/phocadownload/category/359-ncr930-09-2024-appointment-of-a-service-provider-for-periodic-review-of-limitations-on-fees-interest-rates-and-credit-life-insurance-regulations-re-advertisement?download=2145%3Ancr930-09-2024-terms-of-reference-review-of-fees-use); [Tribunal application of the 15% limit](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZANCT/2024/39.html); [SARS VAT](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/).

No later replacement fee schedule was located in this research. The age of the gazette does not itself make it obsolete, but a final currency check is required before launch.

## Assessment design
1. Verify control of an approved institution-specific student email with a one-time link or code. An email suffix alone is insufficient: staff/alumni may have institution addresses. Maintain a reviewed institution/domain registry; verify current registration independently. Define a process for genuine students whose institution does not issue an eligible email.
2. Verify identity and that the payout/collection bank account belongs to the applicant. An ID is an identifier, not a list of debts.
3. Obtain a registered bureau's consumer report through an authorised commercial integration and appropriate notices/lawful permissions. Existing debts and payment history come from reported data, which can be incomplete or delayed. Ask the applicant to disclose additional obligations. Do not promise discovery of every debt.
4. Read three consecutive months of statement data and verify completeness and authenticity. Extract recurring allowance/income, essential expenses, existing debt payments, irregular deposits and returned collections. Reconcile opening/closing balances and duplicates. Do not treat transfers between the applicant's own accounts, borrowed money or one-off gifts as recurring income automatically.
5. Use deterministic, versioned affordability rules with a living-expense buffer and verified debt obligations. The decision must not rely on a language model's invented numbers. Protect restricted-purpose funds, seasonal/term-time income and allowance delays in the policy; do not assume any allowance is available for loan repayments.
6. AI may propose transaction categories and flag discrepancies, with source references and confidence. Send ambiguous statements, uncertain income, suspected fraud and proposed adverse outcomes for meaningful human review. Keep an audit trail of data, corrections, rules, reviewer decisions and reasons.
7. Give applicants understandable reasons and a correction/review path. Avoid inference about religion, health, politics or similar sensitive traits from merchant names. Use only necessary data; separate assessment permissions from optional marketing.

POPIA section 71 restricts solely automated decisions with substantial/legal effects and sets out exceptions and safeguards, including representations and information about underlying logic. A human reviewer must actually assess the evidence, not rubber-stamp the model.

Important distinction: latest three months' statements are Buddy Loans' proposed evidence policy. Regulation 23A(4)'s specific documentary prescription was set aside in Truworths (2018); that did not remove the broader affordability obligation. A 2025 Tribunal decision expressly recognises this distinction. Do not describe the exact four-document checklist as universally mandated by the NCR.

Sources: [TransUnion](https://www.transunion.co.za/client-support/faqs), [POPIA s71](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/2013-004.pdf), [Truworths judgment](https://www3.saflii.org/za/cases/ZAWCHC/2018/41.html), [2025 Tribunal judgment](https://www1.saflii.org/za/cases/ZANCT/2025/38.pdf).

## Repayments and debit mandates
Recommend DebiCheck via an approved collections provider/bank, subject to commercial onboarding. It involves confirmation through the customer's bank. A checkbox in Buddy Loans does not itself authenticate a DebiCheck mandate. Do not request the customer's banking PIN or password.

Show the creditor, exact collection amount, frequency, date and applicable tracking rules before authorisation. Distinguish the loan contract from the collection mandate. Store mandate references and status, not banking credentials. Payout must wait for the policy's required verification and authorisation states.

The founder confirmed a single payment on the next verified allowance date, not monthly instalments. Allowance dates vary, so confirm the date for each agreement. Set maximum permitted duration and a process for delayed allowances; do not infer an unrestricted right to sweep an account or keep retrying after an allowance delay. Publish a missed-payment/contact process and control changes to dates/amounts.

Sources: [SARB](https://resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/oversight-supervision), [PASA](https://pasa.org.za/pasa-resources/pasa-debit-orders/).

- One repayment is confirmed. Define term limits, how the next allowance date is verified, and how holiday/term breaks and allowance delays are handled.
- Select actual interest/fees below or at reviewed ceilings; approve VAT treatment and dated calculation tests.
- Contract bureau, bank-account verification, statement processing and collections providers; verify reporting obligations and provider agreements.
- Approve privacy/retention/access controls, incident process, human review and complaint routes. Before using a third-party AI processor, check retention/training controls, operator agreement, hosting and any cross-border implications.
- Publish the entity/contact information, verified registration, pricing example, terms and privacy notice before opening real applications.
